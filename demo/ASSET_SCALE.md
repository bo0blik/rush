# Asset dimensions

World distances and sprite dimensions use meters. Sprite axes retain the same scale, avoiding horizontal stretching.

- Sedan: 1.9 m wide, 4.5 m long, 1.65 m tall; rear artwork is calibrated to its collision width.
- Truck: 2.4 m wide, 6.4 m long, 2.9 m tall.
- Village homes: 7.2–9 m including roofs; farm homes: 7.4 m.
- Church: 23 m; barn: 9 m; windmill: 13 m.
- Road: 9 m; fence: 1.2 m; roadside posts and signs keep their existing dimensions.
- Trees: 6.5–22 m near the road, 18–32 m on distant slopes.
- Cows: 1.35–1.45 m; sheep: 0.85–0.95 m. Flowers and insects retain small dimensions.

These are gameplay proportions for fictional assets. They are not manufacturer specifications.
