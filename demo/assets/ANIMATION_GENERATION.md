# Expanded animation atlases

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. Reference images provided identity and style. Source PNGs copied intact into the project. Sedan v3 is an imagegen edit to improve spacing; sedan v2 is retained as a source iteration. No API fallback or offline raster edits were used.

## Final assets

- `rider_animation_v2.png`: 1254x1254; six rows of six frames, 36 total. Ride, braking, left lean, right lean, fall, recovery.
- `sedan_animation_v3.png`: 1536x1024; four rows of six frames, 24 total. Rear angles, front angles, rear driving, front driving.
- `truck_animation_v2.png`: 1536x1024; the same 24-frame organization.

The sheets do not exactly follow the requested grid boundaries. `tools/build_animation_resources.py` inspects connected alpha silhouettes without changing pixels, fits AtlasTexture regions and measures bottom contact anchors. Resources are `rider_animation_frames.tres`, `sedan_animation_frames.tres`, `truck_animation_frames.tres`. Never slice them by a uniform grid.

`atlas_visual.gd` and `atlas_animation.gdshader` align frames at their ground anchors and interpolate adjacent poses using alpha-weighted color and nearest sampling. This is sprite pose interpolation, not skeletal animation or a rigid-body crash. AI-drawn adjacent poses still contain small geometry differences.

Current rider artwork height: 1.50 m, width derived from its original aspect ratio. Sedan collision width/length/height: 1.75/4.2/1.5 m; truck: 2.4/6.4/2.9 m. Vehicle artwork is scaled uniformly to its height; true side profiles replace the earlier projected-width stretching. Collision artwork is mirrored by hit direction. Light contact takes 0.8 s; a severe hit takes 2.4 s including fall, ground hold and lifting the bike. Controls recover automatically.
